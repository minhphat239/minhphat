About

A brief introduction.

My name is Minh Phát. I'm in Grade 11 at a regular high school. I don't have any special certifications or fancy awards hanging on my wall. Most days I'm just trying to survive math class.

But when I get home, I open my laptop and start building things. Websites, small scripts, random experiments — anything that catches my attention. Sometimes they work. Most times they don't. That's the fun part.

I'm fascinated by how technology works under the hood. Why does this button make that happen? What happens between me typing a URL and the page loading? These questions keep me up at night.

I'm not trying to be the next prodigy or get into some prestigious university. I'm just a curious kid who genuinely enjoys the process of creating things — even if half of it is spent on Stack Overflow.
